Frequently asked questions

What is included in à la carte?

À la carte florals are designer-selected arrangements in your chosen color palette. You pick the pieces, we pick the flowers. It's a simpler, more affordable way to get beautiful wedding florals without the full-service experience.

Can I request specific flowers?

À la carte is designer's choice within your palette. If you have specific flower requests, our full-service option is the way to go.

Do you deliver?

Yes. Delivery is available throughout the DC, Maryland, and Virginia area. We'll confirm the delivery fee when we confirm your order.

How far in advance should I order?

We recommend ordering at least 3 to 4 weeks before your event. If your date is closer than that, reach out and we'll do our best.

Do you set up on site?

À la carte does not include setup. Everything is delivered ready to place. If you'd like on-site setup and design, check out full service.

Questions? We'd love to hear from you.
